# Support

For reproducible plugin problems, open a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/intermynd-instruments/psvita-usb-audio-midi/issues/new/choose)
and use the bug report form. Include the requested Vita, host, DAW, build, and
diagnostic details.

For early ideas and general project discussion, join the
[Intermynd Instruments Discord](https://discord.gg/SGtAZqPVV).

For Vita modding, VitaShell, or general homebrew installation help, use the
[Vita Hacks Guide](https://vita.hacks.guide/) and its community support
resources. This project's issue tracker is limited to psvita-usb-audio-midi.

Support is provided when time permits. Opening an issue or starting a discussion
does not guarantee a fix, feature, response time, or acceptance of contributed
code.
